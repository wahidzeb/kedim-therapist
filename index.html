About Kedim Therapist

In Kedim Therapist you are Dr. Kedim, a ginger cat with a red scarf who listens to silly animal patients. Each session is a short, funny chat with Kind, Wise or Funny replies, plus one mini-game.

The patient's mood decides your star rating. There are 12 patients with three sessions each, 7 mini-games, a shop for your office and a walk-in clinic.

3 quick tips

  1. Funny is never the best reply. The best answer is always Kind or Wise, so think about what the patient needs.
  2. Decorating the office and the Tea Machine help, because the patient starts happier.
  3. Stuck? The Insight Nap button highlights the best reply. You start with three, so save them.

How to Play

You are Dr. Kedim, a cat therapist with a PhD (in Napping). Silly animal patients need your help!

Sessions

Tap the speech bubble to read on. When it's your turn, pick a reply: 💗 Kind, 😹 Funny or 🦉 Wise. The best answer depends on the patient, so read the room! Silly answers are hilarious but lower the mood meter.

Fill the mood meter to earn up to 5 stars, coins and a glowing review. 2+ stars and the patient comes back next time with a new chapter of their story. After 3 sessions they graduate!

Stuck? Tap 💤 for an Insight Nap: Kedim dozes off and the best answer glows.

Therapy mini-games

🫧Breathe With Me: tap when the ring meets the bubble.
🧶Untangle Thoughts: drag yarn balls until no strands cross.
🃏Feelings Match: flip cards to find matching feelings.
🫙Worry Jar: drag the jar to catch grey worries; let the golden thoughts pass.
🎨Ink Blot Test: pick what you see. The answer that fits the patient gives insight!
😽Purr Therapy: tap when the needle is in the green zone.
🐾Whack-a-Thought: bonk gloomy thoughts, spare the sunny ones.

Your office

Spend coins in the Shop on decorations (they add coziness, so patients arrive in a better mood), outfits and fur colors for Kedim, and handy upgrades like the Tea Machine.

Tap Kedim for wisdom (and purrs). Tap the things on the side table... and see what Kedim does.

Walk-in Clinic

Unlocked after your first sessions: endless random patients with random problems, plus a seeded Daily Patient for bonus coins.

Real talk

Kedim is a cartoon cat, not a doctor. If you're ever struggling, talk to someone you trust. It really helps. 💛
